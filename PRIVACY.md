# Picasso Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** _fill in before publishing (e.g. 2026-12-01)_

Picasso ("the app") is a camera tracing aid. This policy explains what the
app does with your information. Short version: **your work never leaves
your device.**

## Information the app accesses — and where it stays

- **Camera.** The app shows a live camera preview under your reference
  image so you can trace. Video is recorded, and still frames are captured,
  **only when you tap record, timelapse, or before/after** — and the files
  are saved to your device's gallery (Movies/Picasso, Pictures/Picasso).
  Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **Microphone.** Used only inside video recordings you start yourself, and
  only if you grant the permission. You can decline and still record silent
  video. Audio never leaves your device.
- **Photos and files you choose.** Reference images you pick are copied into
  the app's private storage on your device so projects survive restarts.
  Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **AR anchor (optional).** If you enable it, Google Play Services for AR
  processes camera frames **on your device** for motion tracking. That
  processing is governed by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Picasso
  itself sends nothing to Google or anyone else.

## Information the app does NOT collect

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reports, no
location, no contacts. The app works fully offline and talks to no servers.

## Data retention and deletion

Everything Picasso stores lives on your device: projects in private app
storage, exports in your gallery. Delete a project in the app to remove it;
uninstalling the app removes its private storage. Gallery exports are yours
to delete like any photo or video.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes (for example when accounts or cloud features are
added), the updated policy will ship with the app update and the date above
will change.
